A capstone project exploring how AI agents can help software businesses find product knowledge, draft customer email replies, and transform meeting notes into clear scopes of work.

Project status: Early scaffold and documentation. The live AI workflows and integrations are still under development.
Three connected workflows
TASK 01 · PYTHON

Knowledge Brain

Planned

Ingest help-center articles, keep the index fresh, retrieve relevant evidence, and return answers with source citations through an API.

TASK 02 · TYPESCRIPT

Inbox Agent

Planned

Classify customer emails, request facts from the Knowledge Brain, and draft replies for human review and feedback.

TASK 03 · PYTHON

Meeting to Scope

Planned

Analyze meeting transcripts, estimate tasks, prepare a proposal, and highlight changes in follow-up meetings for approval.

Engineering principles

Evidence first

Answers should be grounded in retrieved sources and clearly abstain when evidence is insufficient.

Human approval

Customer replies and proposals remain reviewable, with approval gates for consequential actions.

Measured quality

Evaluation will compare quality, latency, cost, and failure handling rather than relying on unsupported claims.
